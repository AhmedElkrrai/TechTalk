Icon exploration — 01

Three directions for the TechTalk mark

Same idea in every version — a code bracket </> inside a chat bubble, reading "Tech" through "Talk" — built from the colors already living in TechTalkTheme.kt, not invented ones. What changes across the three is how much of that theme the icon carries: a bold two-tone signal, a nod to every subscribed language, or the quietest possible mark.

Cyan Signal

leading pick

A full cyan-to-teal field with the mark knocked out in near-black. Reads instantly at any size and is the most competitive next to typical home-screen icons.

On a home screen

#00F0FF #03DAC6 #0A0A0A

Multi-Tech Ring

option 2

The app's real dark surface, with the bubble outlined in the five subscribed languages' own tag colors. Says "every language, one conversation" — at the cost of some legibility once the icon is small.

On a home screen

#121212 #7F52FF #3DDC84 #F05138 #147EFB #ED8B00

Flat Duotone

option 3

Just the app's secondary teal and its own dark surface, flat, no gradient. The safest choice for legibility at a 40px home-screen size — quietest of the three.

#03DAC6 #121212
